Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

ApexTimeClips is a desktop application that helps a creator prepare short vertical videos from their own live-stream clips and publish them to the creator's own TikTok account. This policy explains what data the application processes.

1. Information we process

  • TikTok authorization data: OAuth access token, refresh token, open_id and display name. The display name is requested only to show which account is connected.
  • Video files and captions that the creator selects or generates locally.
  • Local logs and manifests used to avoid duplicate uploads and to show status.

2. How we use it

  • To authenticate the creator with TikTok using Login Kit.
  • To upload and publish the creator's video through the Content Posting API.
  • To display the connected account and the result of each post.

3. Storage and sharing

All data is stored locally on the creator's computer. ApexTimeClips does not operate a server, does not receive the creator's videos or tokens, and does not sell or share data with third parties. The only external service contacted is the TikTok API, as required to perform the actions requested by the creator.

4. Retention

Tokens remain on the local computer until the creator revokes access in TikTok settings or deletes the local token file. Videos and logs remain until the creator deletes them.

5. Your choices

  • Revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions.
  • Delete local files in the application folder to remove tokens and generated media.

6. Children

The application is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes

We may update this policy when the application changes. The effective date above will be revised.
